The works

Ninety-six metres.
Never off the hook.

Two bays run the full length under 20 MT cranes. A stack shell or a girder travels end to end, from cutting bed to paint booth, without being set down outside.

Covered shed, plan to scale. Crane positions indicative.

From plate to primer.

The H-beam and built-up section line, in order.

STEP 1

Cut

CNC plasma and flame profiling on a 4 × 15 m bed.

STEP 2

Assemble

Automated fit-up of webs and flanges, up to 2 m deep.

STEP 3

Weld

Submerged-arc welding at 1,000 A and 1,250 A.

STEP 4

Straighten

Hydraulic flange straightening, flanges to 80 mm.

STEP 5

Drill

CNC multi-axis drilling to drawing.

STEP 6

Finish

Blast, airless spray, DFT checked before dispatch.
