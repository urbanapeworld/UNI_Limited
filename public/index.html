    About Our Company

    Heavy Fabrication for India's Core Industries

    Uni Profiles Pvt. Ltd., incorporated in 2009, runs a steel and alloy-steel fabrication works at Rajgangpur, Odisha, 40 km from Rourkela. We make heavy steel equipment and boiler auxiliary structures for BHEL, SAIL, Thermax, L&T, Tata Projects and other leading engineering companies, with an annual capacity of 6,000 MT.

    Our Facility ↗
    6,000 MT a year across two 17 × 96 m bays, served by 2 × 20 MT EOT cranes, a 600 MT hydraulic press and a full H-beam line.
    ISO 9001:2015 certified. Stage inspection from material inward to dispatch, with certified NDT and blasting-painting inspectors on our team.
    Five acres off SH-10 at Rajgangpur, Sundargarh district: 40 km from Rourkela and 70 km from Jharsuguda airport, with open-yard trailer loading.

    Products

    Fabricated to Your Drawing. Delivered to Your Schedule.

    Boiler & Conveyor Structures

    Buck stays, columns, beams, bracings and conveyor galleries for utility boilers, coke-oven batteries and material handling.

    Stacks & Chimneys

    Self-supporting steel stacks in rolled shells with flanged joints, platforms and ladders, painted and banded for dispatch.

    Fired Heater Non-Pressure Parts

    Radiant and convection casings, structural frames and ducting, trial-assembled at the works before shipment.

    Chutes, Hoppers & Ducts

    Stiffened plate work for crushers and bunkers, large flanged flue ducts and gas collecting mains.

    Non-Pressurised Tanks

    Overhead, drainage and process tanks, and large stiffened rectangular bath tanks.

    Bridge Girders & Built-up Sections

    RDSO-approved open web, plate and composite girders, trial-assembled in our yard, plus H-beams and built-up sections from the automated beam line.
